## C1 (Critical) — the emulator-walk gate: CLOSED, both arms driven

### What was actually wrong, and it is worse than "an item was deferred"

F-181 said S2's emulator gate was blocked on driving the on-device keyboard. The
blocker did not exist: the emulator payload has always carried a `ClassMnemonic`
(master A, `cmd/emu/sysw_cards_payload.go`), so the self seed arrives **from the
payload with confirm-taps only**, and both arms the plan names are tap-only. The
plan's own words were *"default taps + **payload seed**"*.

I had turned **"the route I picked is hard" into "the gate is blocked"**. The
worse half was the justification, not the deferral: F-181 claimed the gate was
*"satisfied by the Go walk plus the payload-leg emulator walk that RAN green"* —
and that walk stops at `waitFor("Input Seed")` by design and had never reached an
engrave. **I reported a gate closed from a walk that could not close it.**
Withdrawn the way F-176 was, with the false sentence quoted rather than deleted.

### What was built

`cmd/emu/walk_build_policy.js` gains:

- a **screen-driven picker** taking per-card `use`/`skip`, which stops when the
  remaining-equals-needed short-circuit takes over — a tap-counting loop would
  have carried on tapping CONFIRM into whatever came next;
- a **payload-seed leg** (`seedFrom: "payload"`), which taps the row rather than
  trusting the default, because a default that silently moves is how a walk ends
  up driving the keyboard while reporting it drove the payload;
- a **refusal fork** (`raceFor`), because waiting on one needle and reading a
  timeout as "the other arm happened" reports the wrong arm whenever the flow
  simply hangs;
- an **engrave tail** reusing `walk_trace_a.js`'s measured machinery (toolpath
  progress, wall-clock hold, unknown-screen stop);
- `ok` that **names its outcome**: an engrave run is green only when the census
  holds the DERIVED plate count with nothing unattributed (F-170).
