### `f92bb02` — F-435, the body budget

`EngraveText` reads the **same `yBudget`** the free-text path wraps against, at

measurement as the refusal's non-vacuity**: with the footer taken away the same

## 2. One deliberate departure from the brief, and why

**The budget binds only where something is cut at the limit — the footer.**

The faithful `yBudget` mirror (refuse whenever `offy > limit`, footer or not) was
implemented first and **reverted after the suite refused a shipped golden**:

```
--- FAIL: TestText/0-shards-1
    EngraveText: backup: text does not fit a plate: the body ends at 529920, past the 524800 it is budgeted to
```

Without a footer, `limit` is the bottom margin — and that y is already enforced,
*better*, by `gui.toPlate`, which measures the **ink** rather than the nominal
row. The difference is the last row's descender space and it is real capacity:
`backup/testdata/text-0-shards-1.bin` ends 5120 units past the margin nominally
and short of it in ink. Refusing there would also silently change which variants
`validateDescriptor` / `validateMdmk` offer — the exact thing the unbounded wrap
in that function exists to avoid.

The footer is different in kind: it sits **inside** the safety margin, so ink on
top of it is still `In()` the plate and `toPlate` cannot see the collision at
all. There is no downstream check to defer to, which is why that one is here.

Final guard, `backup/backup.go:526`:

```go
if plate.Footer != "" && offy > limit {
```

The reasoning is recorded at the check and in the test, not only here.

   `bundlePlateTextFits` only ever saw text-only paragraphs — so nothing
