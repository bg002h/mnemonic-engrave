=== 12. RESTORE FROM THE PLATES ===

Everything above starts from the strings in out/. An operator starts from
a stack of plates. restore_from_plates.py goes the other way: it decodes
the QR out of each rendered plate image with zbarimg -- an independent
decoder, not the library that drew them -- and rebuilds the wallet from
what came off the images and nothing else. Two layers: the QR matches the
manifest string byte-for-byte, and the rebuilt template-id matches what
this run derived by a separate route.

$ python3 /scratch/code/shibboleth/mnemonic-engrave/design/journeys/restore_from_plates.py tr-pathological
tr-pathological: 37 plates in the manifest

Layer 1 — the QR on each plate decodes to the string the manifest names:
  36 of 37 plates decoded byte-exact via zbarimg
  1 plate(s) carry NO rendered image and were NOT checked:
    plate 37 (ms1)

Rebuilding from the plate-borne strings alone: 6 md1 chunk(s), 30 mk1 string(s)

Layer 2 — the rebuilt wallet, against what the journey derived separately:
  template-id off the PLATES : 44ad26a19b53048b6ff8957359a30c31
  template-id from the run   : 44ad26a19b53048b6ff8957359a30c31

  slots 11, distinct declarations 11, with a fingerprint 11
  the plate-borne template is SEATABLE

RESTORED FROM THE PLATES. 36 images in, the same wallet out.
Caveat: 1 plate(s) had no rendered image and were not part of this.
Not metal: this proves the software chain, not that steel is legible.
[exit 0]

AND THE GATE CAN FAIL. A match check nobody has broken is not evidence.

codex32 checksum at decode, the second is perfectly valid and simply the
wrong wallet, which is the only one that reaches the id comparison:
