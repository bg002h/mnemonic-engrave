=== THE REDUNDANCY QUESTION ===
  fountain.go:242 — parts 1..seqLen are SINGLETONS (one pure fragment each).
  Only seqNum > seqLen produces mixed parts. So emitting exactly seqLen
  parts gives ZERO redundancy: lose one and the message is unrecoverable.
  Decoder::Progress() estimates seqLen * 1.75 parts for RANDOM reception.

  policy                            parts     survives   cost vs bare
  exactly seqLen (singletons)    seqLen+0      no loss              -
  seqLen + 1                     seqLen+1   any 1 lost     +1 symbols
  seqLen + 2                     seqLen+2   any 2 lost     +2 symbols
